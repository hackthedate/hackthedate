## Find the cybersecurity events that matter to you

The cybersecurity world never stops moving.

New CTF competitions, conferences, workshops, training programs, meetups, webinars, hackathons, and competitions are happening around the world every day.

Finding the right ones should not require searching across dozens of websites, communities, social networks, and event pages.

**HackTheDate brings cybersecurity events together in one place.**

Explore upcoming events, discover new opportunities, and find experiences that match your interests, location, schedule, and skill level.

<br>

## Why HackTheDate?

### Global discovery

Discover cybersecurity events from communities and organizers around the world.

### Multiple event types

Explore CTFs, conferences, workshops, training, meetups, webinars, hackathons, competitions, and more from one platform.

### Online and in-person events

Find opportunities regardless of where you are.

### Better event discovery

Search and explore cybersecurity events without jumping between dozens of different websites and platforms.

### Built for the cybersecurity community

HackTheDate connects people looking for cybersecurity opportunities with the organizers creating them.

### Easy event submission

Organizers can submit cybersecurity events directly to the platform and make them discoverable by the community.
